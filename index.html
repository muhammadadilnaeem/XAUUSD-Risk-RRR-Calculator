Formula & Calculation Guide
Reference Library
📏
Risk Distance & Reward Distance
Raw price distance from entry to SL / TP
Risk Distance = |Entry − Stop Loss| Reward Distance = |Take Profit − Entry|
Absolute value so the formula works for both LONG and SHORT. For LONG: Entry > SL and TP > Entry. For SHORT: Entry < SL and TP < Entry.
VariableMeaning
EntryTrade open price
SLStop Loss — exit point if trade moves against you
TPTake Profit — target exit to lock in profit
💵
Risk & Reward in Dollars — Universal Formula
Works for XAUUSD and all 10 forex pairs
Value in Quote Currency = Distance × Contract Units × Lot Size Risk ($) = (Risk Distance × Contract Units × Lot) ÷ Conversion Factor Reward ($) = (Reward Distance × Contract Units × Lot) ÷ Conversion Factor
Contract Units = 100 oz for XAUUSD, or 100,000 units for standard forex lots.
Conversion Factor depends on the instrument's category:
CategoryExample PairsConversion Factor
MetalXAUUSD1 (already USD)
USD-QuoteEUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD1 (already USD)
USD-BaseUSD/JPY, USD/CAD, USD/CHFDivide by Entry Price
Cross (GBP quote)EUR/GBPMultiply by GBP/USD rate
Cross (JPY quote)GBP/JPY, EUR/JPYDivide by USD/JPY rate
Why this matters: A pip on GBP/JPY isn't worth the same in USD as a pip on EUR/USD. The calculator applies the correct factor automatically based on the instrument you select — you only need to supply a conversion rate for cross pairs (a sensible default is pre-filled).
📊
Risk Percentage
Portion of account balance being risked
Risk (%) = ( Risk ($) ÷ Account Balance ) × 100
What fraction of your capital is at stake on this trade. Professional traders consider this the most important number — it governs long-term survival.
⚖️
Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR)
How much you gain vs how much you risk per trade
RRR = Reward ($) ÷ Risk ($) → expressed as 1 : RRR
RRR of 2.0 means "for every $1 risked, you stand to make $2." Always expressed with Risk as 1.
Why it matters: With 1:2 RRR you only need to win 34% of trades to break even. With 1:3 that drops to 25%.

Reverse-Engineering Lot Size
Find the correct lot size for a fixed risk amount
Max Risk ($) = Account Balance × (Risk % ÷ 100) Lot Size = Max Risk ($) ÷ (SL in Pips × Pip Value per Standard Lot)
Decide your risk % first, then solve for lot size — never the other way around. The "Lot Size Calculator" tool above does this automatically for whichever instrument is selected.
💱
Why Conversion Rates Matter
USD-quote pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, NZD/USD) and XAUUSD already price in USD, so no conversion is needed. USD-base pairs (USD/JPY, USD/CAD, USD/CHF) automatically use your entered Entry Price to convert. True cross pairs (EUR/GBP, GBP/JPY, EUR/JPY) need one extra number — a live conversion rate — because neither side of the pair is USD. The app pre-fills a realistic default, but for real trading always plug in the live rate from your broker.

Worked Example 1 — XAUUSD
Metal category, conversion factor = 1
Given: Account Balance = $1,000.00 Lot Size = 0.01 Entry Price = 2000.00 Stop Loss (SL) = 1995.00 Take Profit (TP) = 2010.00 Risk Distance = |2000.00 − 1995.00| = 5.00 Reward Distance = |2010.00 − 2000.00| = 10.00 $ per unit distance = 0.01 lot × 100 oz = $1.00 Risk ($) = 5.00 × $1.00 = $5.00 Reward ($) = 10.00 × $1.00 = $10.00 Risk (%) = ($5.00 ÷ $1,000.00) × 100 = 0.50% RRR = $10.00 ÷ $5.00 = 1 : 2.00
✅ 0.50% risk, 1:2 RRR — a well-structured, low-risk trade.
📖
Worked Example 2 — EUR/USD
USD-Quote category, conversion factor = 1
Given: Account Balance = $2,000.00 Lot Size = 0.10 (mini lot) Entry Price = 1.16250 Stop Loss (SL) = 1.16000 Take Profit (TP) = 1.16750 Risk Distance = |1.16250 − 1.16000| = 0.00250 Reward Distance = |1.16750 − 1.16250| = 0.00500 $ per unit distance = 0.10 lot × 100,000 = $10,000 Risk ($) = 0.00250 × 10,000 = $25.00 Reward ($) = 0.00500 × 10,000 = $50.00 Risk (%) = ($25.00 ÷ $2,000.00) × 100 = 1.25% RRR = $50.00 ÷ $25.00 = 1 : 2.00
⚠️ 1.25% risk is moderate — still inside acceptable range, but reduce lot size for a more conservative trade.
📖
Worked Example 3 — USD/JPY
USD-Base category, conversion factor = ÷ Entry Price
Given: Account Balance = $5,000.00 Lot Size = 0.20 Entry Price = 154.000 Stop Loss (SL) = 153.700 Take Profit (TP) = 154.600 Risk Distance = |154.000 − 153.700| = 0.300 Reward Distance = |154.600 − 154.000| = 0.600 Value in JPY = Distance × 100,000 × 0.20 lot Risk (JPY) = 0.300 × 100,000 × 0.20 = 6,000 JPY Reward (JPY) = 0.600 × 100,000 × 0.20 = 12,000 JPY Convert JPY → USD by dividing by Entry Price (154.000): Risk ($) = 6,000 ÷ 154.000 = $38.96 Reward ($) = 12,000 ÷ 154.000 = $77.92 Risk (%) = ($38.96 ÷ $5,000.00) × 100 = 0.78% RRR = 1 : 2.00
✅ 0.78% risk, 1:2 RRR — the app performs this JPY→USD conversion automatically.
📊 VSA Method 🎯 High Probability Setups 📈 80–90% Win Rate 🔗 Trend-Following
🏹 The VSA Partial-Close Strategy
In VSA (Volume Spread Analysis) we work with high-probability, trend-aligned setups that carry an 80–90% win rate. Because the edge is so strong, you may take up to 4% risk on these setups instead of the usual 1–2%.

When price hits your first target (see table below — pip counts use whichever instrument you're trading):
→ Close 80% of your position — locking in 3% profit and making the trade risk-free.
→ Move SL to Entry — the remaining 20% now rides for free.
→ Let the remaining 1% run toward the previous High (bullish) or previous Low (bearish).

This way you always bank profit, yet still participate in the full trend move with zero further risk.

🚨 Critical Risk Warnings — Always Remember
1. Trade going against you? Close immediately. Every pip of hesitation costs you more.
2. Forgot to set your SL? Close the trade NOW. An account without a SL can go to zero or negative balance (margin call / liquidation).
3. These are profit-scenario targets only. If the move fails before your first TP, honour your SL without exception.
4. Never move SL further away from your entry. Only move it to break-even once in profit.
5. No trade is worth your account. Capital preservation always comes first.
